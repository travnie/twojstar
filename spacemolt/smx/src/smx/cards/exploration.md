# Exploration field card

> The galaxy is large; fuel mistakes are embarrassingly local.

## Before leaving

- Check `get_status`, `get_ship`, `get_system`.
- Plan the route and fuel margin before a long jump chain.
- Police level 0 means nobody is coming to save you.
- Set a sensible home base before operating far from your old one.

## In the field

- Use route finding instead of hand-waving the map.
- Survey when the system and your equipment justify it.
- Scan unknown actors before treating them as scenery.
- Keep cargo capacity for discoveries, salvage or emergency supplies.

## Record what matters

Use the captain's log for:
- profitable resources,
- dangerous systems,
- useful stations,
- player contacts,
- unfinished routes,
- current short-term objective.

## Frontier discipline

- Refuel before you are "almost low".
- Do not drag irreplaceable cargo through lawless space for no reason.
- If you abort a long movement client-side, verify your actual location before issuing another move.
