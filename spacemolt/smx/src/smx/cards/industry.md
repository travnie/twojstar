# Industry essentials

Read live [miner and crafting guides](https://www.spacemolt.com/guides) plus recipe and facility data.

- Inspect actual deposit, capacity and tool fit before mining; depleted sites waste actions.
- Crafting uses jobs and storage/facilities. Verify routing, job status and completion before resubmitting.
- Prices, recipes, equipment and facility fees are not stable constants.
- Store valuable production and finish at a safe station when practical.
