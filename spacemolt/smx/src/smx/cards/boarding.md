# Boarding field card

> Boarding is high-value and high-friction. Use the live `boarding` guide for exact current thresholds and hull bonuses.

## Setup

- You need boarding capability plus fit marines.
- Boarding commits the ship: weapons are suppressed and defensive stance benefits are reduced.
- Get the target close and keep its shields below the current boarding threshold.

## During the fight

- Use the board stance once conditions are favorable.
- Boarding links are contested. Another same-tick attempt involving either hull can reject yours.
- Do not casually destroy the hull you are trying to steal. That kills the prize and can kill your marines.
- Personnel losses matter. A ship without enough fit crew may stop functioning.

## Capture does not mean "teleported to garage"

- A successful capture becomes an intact prize at the battle location.
- Claim it and send it to an accessible station.
- Recovery consumes fuel and the prize can stall, be intercepted or be recaptured.
- Use prize service actions to refuel, repair, stop, resume or redirect when needed.

## Personnel logistics

- Keep enough fit crew on your active ship when assigning prize crew.
- Recruit and treat personnel at suitable station services.
- Field medical and allied personnel-transfer options can rescue an operation, but are not magic infinite crew fountains.

## Rule of thumb

Capture only when the hull, modules or cargo justify the recovery chain. Otherwise wreck salvage may be faster and safer.
