About Doc Bench · supported files and privacy

Which documents can I open?

Doc Bench opens UTF-8 text files even when their extensions are unfamiliar. It recognizes Markdown, JSON-family formats, YAML, XML, common config and script files, and offers previews or validation where supported.

What PDF operations are available?

Preview, merge, split, reorder and extract pages; edit bookmarks, metadata and attachments; optimize PDFs and convert to or from DOCX. PDF-to-DOCX is structure-first, not a pixel-perfect reproduction.

What does Files support?

The Files workspace inspects local headers, metadata, text and native media previews, and lists ZIP entries without extracting them. Modern formats may need a browser codec or a future decoder.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Editing and PDF operations run locally in your browser. Saving directly to a local file depends on browser support; downloading an edited copy is the fallback. Read the feature details.
