# Industry field card

> Recipes, facility routing and inputs evolve. Use live catalog/guide output for exact numbers.

## Mining

- Check the POI resource state before wasting ticks.
- Oversized mining arrays may be inefficient or unusable on depleted deposits.
- Mining trains the relevant skill automatically.

## Crafting

- Inspect the live recipe catalog first.
- In current v2, crafting jobs consume inputs from station storage, not loose cargo.
- Use a dry-run quote before expensive jobs.
- Real facilities keep working while you leave; workshop hand-crafting may pause when you undock.
- Avoid duplicating a job just because output has not appeared yet. Crafting completes over later ticks.

## Facility choice

- Fastest, cheapest and prefer-own routing can produce different answers.
- Public facilities may charge rental fees.
- Faction and allied facilities can be strategically better than raw speed.

## Scaling

Mining → refining → crafting is a natural industrial loop, but market demand should decide what deserves mass production.
