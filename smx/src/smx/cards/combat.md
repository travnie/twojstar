# Combat field card

> Compact local reference. Live `help`, battle state and server guides always win if mechanics changed.

## Before shooting

- Check `get_ship`: weapons, ammo, fit and speed.
- Check `get_status`: hull, shields, fuel and current commitments.
- Check `get_system`: police level and whether you are volunteering for an expensive lesson.
- Carry spare ammo. Decide your exit condition before the first shot.

## Range and positioning

- Battles use Outer → Mid → Inner → Engaged rings.
- If your reach is longer, create distance. If your guns are short-range, close hard.
- Speed affects tracking, maneuvering and escape.
- `get_battle_status` is your main per-tick dashboard.

## Damage cheat sheet

- **Thermal**: strong into armor.
- **Void**: bypasses shields, but armor resists it heavily.
- **EM**: control tool against speed and damage output.
- **Energy / explosive**: broad all-round choices.
- Do not pick damage by raw number alone. Match the target's tank.

## Survive the fight

- `brace`: buy time and regenerate shields.
- `evade`: reduce incoming punishment while repositioning or escaping.
- `flee`: start early. Tackle can stop progress.
- If warp-disrupted, remove the tackle source first.
- In fleets: focus fire. Typical priority is logistics → tackle → DPS.

## Aftermath

- Wrecks are valuable and persist. Note the location if you cannot salvage now.
- Repair, reload, refuel and re-check insurance before the next risky sortie.
